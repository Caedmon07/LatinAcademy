1 · THE LANGUAGE OF ROME

What is Latin?

Latin began as the language of ancient Rome. As Roman power expanded, Latin spread through much of Europe, North Africa and the eastern Mediterranean.

It was used in government, law, trade, the army and literature. Even after the western Roman Empire declined, Latin remained a major written language of education, religion, diplomacy, science and scholarship.

2 · WHY LATIN MATTERS

Why learn Latin?

🧠

Think precisely

Latin develops close reading, pattern recognition and logical analysis.

📚

Strengthen English

Latin roots help explain the meaning and structure of many English words.

🌍

Learn other languages

Latin provides a foundation for understanding Romance-language vocabulary and grammar.

⚖️

Understand law

Legal English still uses many Latin expressions and concepts.

🔬

Read science

Latin appears throughout scientific names and technical terminology.

🏛️

Enter the ancient world

Latin opens the door to Roman history, inscriptions, poetry and prose.

4 · THE ROMANCE LANGUAGE FAMILY

Latin’s linguistic descendants

Italian, Spanish, Portuguese, French, Romanian and Catalan developed from varieties of spoken Latin. They are called Romance languages because they descend from the language of Rome.
