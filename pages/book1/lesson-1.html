BOOK I · CHAPTER 1 · LESSON 1

What is a Verb?

Every sentence needs something to happen. The word that expresses that action or state is called a verb.

⏱ 10–15 minutes ⭐ 25 XP 🏛 Beginner

1 · ACTION WORDS

What is happening?

A verb tells us what someone or something does.

🏃

The runner moves quickly.

Which word expresses the action?

2 · FIND THE VERB

Verbs inside sentences

Choose the word that tells us what happens.

The soldier fights.

3 · PERSON

Who is doing the action?

Latin changes a verb ending to identify the person performing the action.

1st

I / we

The speaker acts.

2nd

you / you all

The person addressed acts.

3rd

he, she, it / they

Someone else acts.

Which person includes we?

4 · NUMBER

One or more than one?

🧍

Singular

One person or thing.

🧍🧍🧍

Plural

More than one person or thing.

Is they singular or plural?

5 · YOUR FIRST LATIN VERB

Meet amō

amō means I love or I like.

am- + -ō = amō

The stem carries the central idea: love.

The ending tells us who performs the action: I.
