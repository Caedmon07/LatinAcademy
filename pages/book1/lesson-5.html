1 · COMPLETED PAST

Perfect or imperfect?

The imperfect describes what was happening; the perfect describes what happened and was completed.

IMPERFECTamābat

he was loving / used to love

PERFECTamāvit

he loved / has loved

Which sentence describes a completed action?
